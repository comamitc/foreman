### Procedure trace refresh

When an intentional process change rewords, moves, adds, or removes owner text that [.foreman/traces/happy-path.md](.foreman/traces/happy-path.md) or [.foreman/traces/illegal-paths.md](.foreman/traces/illegal-paths.md) quotes, or adds or removes a row of [references/contract.md](references/contract.md) ("## Illegal combinations"):

1. Find the trace lines to update: run `git grep -n -F '<owner path>' -- .foreman/traces` for each owner file the diff changes, and compare each fenced quote under a matching Owner line with the owner's new text at that line's anchor. Each quote the owner no longer holds verbatim, and each Owner line whose anchor the owner no longer holds, is a line to update.
2. Re-copy each one from the owner: open the owner at the anchor, copy the shape's new text into the fenced block as one line, character for character, and copy the anchor again from the owner's text into the Owner line. When the change adds or removes a shape the happy path produces, or moves it to another station, add, remove, or move that entry in its station section. When the change adds or removes an `## Illegal combinations` row, add or remove its `## IC-N` entry in [.foreman/traces/illegal-paths.md](.foreman/traces/illegal-paths.md). For [.foreman/traces/illegal-paths.md](.foreman/traces/illegal-paths.md), copy instead the owner lines that carry the rule sentence into the fenced block: one or more consecutive whole owner lines, each character for character without its leading indentation, inside the owner section its heading anchor names (the heading through the line before the next heading of the same or higher level) or within five owner lines of a line holding its phrase anchor.
3. Commit the owner change and the trace change on the same branch, so the pull request's diff shows the owner's removed and added lines and the trace's removed and added lines in the same diff.

### Examples

Each entry applies one row above to a real past review finding.

- **Restated rule.**
  - Source: #343 PR2 @ 3a72e21, https://github.com/comamitc/capstan/issues/343#issuecomment-6422757021.
  - Defect: next to its owner link, the plan's new contract section says a forbidden label combination "is repaired from evidence under" Stage projection.
  - Report: the sentence states behavior that its owner section already states; the owner link does not exempt it.
  - Corrected: the new section states its check timing and reporting, and names Stage projection without the repair clause.
- **Misattributed owner.**
  - Source: #331 PR4 @ 4eff6d4, https://github.com/comamitc/capstan/issues/331#issuecomment-6322228170.
  - Defect: a planned sentence cites one owner for "that run and its order"; that owner's cited text delegates the order to a second owner.
  - Report: the pointer cites the first owner for an order rule its text does not state.
  - Corrected: the order pointer names the second owner, and the run pointer keeps the first.
- **Citation without stable anchor.**
  - Source: #304 PR1-03 @ ed8962f, https://github.com/comamitc/capstan/issues/304#issuecomment-6367154828.
  - Defect: a plan quotes the anchor `Every new check fails on a real past occurrence, … and passes on the fix`; the owner holds an occurrence-head and scratch-reproduction clause between the two fragments.
  - Report: the anchor is shortened, and the target does not hold it verbatim.
  - Corrected: `references/modes/correction.md` ("Every new check fails on a real past occurrence, at that occurrence's head or in a scratch reproduction, and passes on the fix."), contiguous text copied from the owner.
- **Dropped inventory or case.**
  - Source: #342 PR4 @ d98d6b1, https://github.com/comamitc/capstan/issues/342#issuecomment-6419670689.
  - Defect: a planned row gives review its drift duty only for a plan or diff "that changes the trace or a file it quotes"; the criterion has no such limit.
  - Report: the dropped case, a stale trace when the reviewed diff changes neither the trace nor a file it quotes.
  - Corrected: the duty applies "For every plan and diff it reviews", as the **Procedure trace.** row now reads.
- **Pack source gap.**
  - Source: #332 PR4 @ 6f5114b, https://github.com/comamitc/capstan/issues/332#issuecomment-6323460780.
  - Defect: the plan-review pack named a `claude.md` range that does not hold the anchor the plan quotes, "This file owns the write-station grant".
  - Report: the range stops short of the sentence the reviewer needs.
  - Corrected: a Named range that holds "This file owns the write-station grant" and its governing text.
- **Non-discriminating evidence check.**
  - Source: #343 PR6 @ 3a72e21, https://github.com/comamitc/capstan/issues/343#issuecomment-6422757021.
  - Defect: G3 "searches the complete owner separately for `anc` and `quote`", so a trace quote that sits elsewhere in the owner file, away from its cited anchor, passes.
  - Report: no planted defect moves only that quote away from its anchor, and the check still passes on that plant.
  - Corrected: a G3 that "distinguishes a quote at the cited anchor from a quote elsewhere in the file", bitten with that plant.
